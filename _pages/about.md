My name is Rogers, which is the English name given by my parents following the famous humanistic psychologist Carl Rogers. I go by it because it's easier to pronounce than my Chinese name Hàochōng (昊翀). Cited from the ancient Chinese philosophy, Hao (昊) means the vast sky, and Chong (翀) means bird soaring upwards.

I will be starting my PhD in Operations Management and Statistics at Rotman School of Management, University of Toronto in September 2025. I'm currently pursuing an Honours Bachelor of Science in Statistics and Computer Science at the University of Toronto. 

My interest lies in applying traditional statistical methods and advanced AI methods to modeling and solving real-world problems.
